How I work

Between the business and the tech. I sit with SDR leaders to find where reps lose time or pipeline, write the requirement for Revenue Systems and GTM Engineering, test the tool myself, pilot it with one pod, then scale it with Enablement once the numbers hold.

  1. 1
    Find the leakFunnel and rep-activity data, plus a ride-along with the reps who feel it.
  2. 2
    Write the requirementA business case, a success metric and an owner, in a form the tech team can build from.
  3. 3
    Try it first-handI configure and test new tools myself before asking reps to change how they work.
  4. 4
    Pilot, then scaleOne pod, a control group, a fixed review date. Scale only what beats the baseline.

BPlaybook

Two lines, one handoff

Outbound and inbound run on separate lines and meet at qualification. Select a station to see the standard I would set: owner, time limit, rule, what gets automated, and how we measure it.

Qualification ladder

One set of definitions for every region, agreed with AEs and Marketing. A meeting only counts when the AE accepts it.

Target

In ICP, owned by a pod, not in an open opportunity.

Exit: not ICP · duplicate
Working

In a sequence or routed from inbound. First touch logged.

Exit: no response after full cadence
Engaged

Two-way conversation with a buyer or influencer.

Exit: wrong person · no budget · timing > 12 months
Meeting set

Discovery booked. Pain, role and timing written in the handoff note.

Exit: no-show twice
AE accepted

AE confirms within 48 h against five criteria. This is the SDR's number.

Exit: rejected with a reason code

Rules of engagement

Who works which account, written down so nobody argues about it on a Friday.

  1. G
    Global accounts go to the Global pod2,000+ employees or 6+ countries. Regional SDRs hand over on detection.
  2. R
    Everyone else follows the regionEMEA or North America, by headquarters country.
  3. O
    Open opportunity winsOutbound signals are paused; the AE gets them as deal intelligence.
  4. 14
    14-day cooldownNo new sequence on a contact touched in the last two weeks.
  5. 48
    Conflicts closed in 48 hoursRaised in one channel, decided by RevOps on the written rules, logged for the quarterly review.

Operating cadence

The meetings I have designed and run for SDR teams, and the decisions each one exists to make.

Daily 15 min

  • Pod stand-upToday's priority accounts, inbound queue, blockers.
  • Inbound SLA checkAny lead older than 5 minutes gets an owner now.

Weekly per pod

  • 1:1 with each SDRTwo emails and one call reviewed, one thing to change.
  • SDR–AE handoff reviewAccepted vs rejected meetings and the reasons.
  • Pipeline checkMeetings booked against target by signal.

Monthly leaders

  • Funnel reviewReply and acceptance by sequence; retire the weakest one.
  • Tool adoptionUsage, licences, data quality per tool.
  • Pilot read-outsKeep, change or stop.

Quarterly with Sales, Mktg, Finance

  • ICP and territoriesSignals, pods and capacity reset.
  • Playbook reissueChangelog shared with Enablement.
  • Stack and budgetRenew, consolidate or cut.
