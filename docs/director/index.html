Three bets

01

Cross-sell is the growth engine

Travel customers without Spend are the warmest pipeline Perk has. Perk's own booking data shows who they are and how much they spend.

02

First-party signals are the moat

Bookings, spend volume, admins leaving, support load. Competitors can buy intent data. They can't buy Perk's product data.

03

Build on what already works

Salesforce, Data Cloud and Agentforce are in place. The job is orchestration, data quality and measurement around them.

5 · Plan

Prove it somewhere, then scale it everywhere

The first 90 days, the business case, how the team builds safely, and which tools earn their place.

Days 1–30 · Listen and audit

Baseline

  • Ride along with SDR, AE and AM teams in EMEA and NA
  • Audit stack, data quality and workflows
  • Time study; yield and latency per funnel stage
  • Pick 2–3 plays with the VP RevOps and Sales VPs
Days 31–60 · Build with supervision

First plays in shadow mode

  • Spend cross-sell from Perk product data
  • AE meeting prep and CRM autofill in one region
  • Git, tests, evals and run logs in place
  • Holdout groups defined before launch
Days 61–90 · Prove and scale

Production and roadmap

  • Promote plays that beat control; stop the rest
  • First impact readout in pipeline and hours
  • Tooling decisions with payback cases
  • Next-quarter roadmap and hiring plan

Engineering standards

Hundreds of sellers will rely on this, so plays ship like software.

Version control

Weights, signal contracts, routing rules, prompts and SOQL in git. Every change is a reviewed PR.

Shadow mode first

New plays run on a holdout segment and go live only when they beat the control group.

Tests and evals

Unit tests on decision logic. Eval sets for every AI output: accuracy, tone, no invented facts.

No duplicates

A stable action ID is the upsert key, so a retried job never creates a second task or enrollment.

Visible failures

Enrichment misses, low AI confidence and API errors land in an exception queue with an owner.

Logs and rollback

Every run logs volume, coverage, cost and errors. Config snapshots allow a one-step rollback.

Stack decisions

Hypotheses to test in the audit. Only Salesforce, Data Cloud, Agentforce and Slack are confirmed publicly.
